How to Play

W A S D move   Shift sprint   Space jump   Mouse look
E pick up broccoli and candy   1 feed broccoli   2 feed candy
Esc pause   M mute   1-5 battle actions

Goal: raise your dragon to the highest level you can. At level 100 it awakens as Ice (water), Metal (earth) or Lightning (fire).

Candy gives XP and speed, but gorge on it and your dragon suffers a sugar crash and loses levels.

Broccoli builds health and strength, but feed it too much and the dragon will eat you.

The meters beside each food show how stuffed your dragon is. The fuller it gets, the riskier the next bite. They drain over time.

Wild dragons roam the land. Beat them in turn-based battles to gain XP. Water beats fire, fire beats earth, earth beats water.
